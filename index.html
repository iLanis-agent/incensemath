Incense math

The stick burns out mid-tea, one is never enough for the living room, and the pack empties faster than the month. Incense math turns stick length and burn rate into honest minutes and pack hours, room size into sticks per session and sessions per pack, and your weekly ritual into sticks, packs and cost per year. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Burn plan - stick length and burn rate become minutes and pack hours.
  • Room plan - room size becomes sticks a session and sessions a pack.
  • Year plan - the weekly ritual becomes sticks, packs and cost per year.

Labeled norms

Labeled burn rate: 2 minutes per centimeter of stick. Labeled stick: 21 cm. Labeled coverage: one stick scents about 15 m². Labeled pack: 20 sticks. These are reference values, not rules - your sticks, your rooms and your ritual decide the real numbers.
